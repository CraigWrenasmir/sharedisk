Music Box

Two iPads, end to end on a table, each of you at your own edge. Play a key and a pin is set into the ring between you. When it comes round to the other side, their wooden hands play it. Nothing sounds until you are both holding a key.

Keep the screen awake: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.
